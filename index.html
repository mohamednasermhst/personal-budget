P2P Lending

Informal loans between you and another person, in either direction. Both the loan itself and its repayments flow into your forecast: money you borrow arrives as income on the loan date and its repayments become expenses, while money you lend leaves as an expense and comes back as income. Only payments you actually record reduce a balance — nothing is assumed settled because a date has passed.

Ad-hoc Savings Spending

One-off purchases paid for out of savings rather than regular income. Each entry reduces the Saving Account Balance from its date onward, and leaves your income, expenses, and Amount to Save untouched.
